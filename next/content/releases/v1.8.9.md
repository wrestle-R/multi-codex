# Multi Codex v1.8.9

## Quit on Mac

Dock Quit and Command-Q exit Multi Codex. The red close button hides the launcher and leaves the menu bar running. You can reopen the launcher from the menu bar or Dock.

## Copy chats and projects from regular Codex

Stop the destination profile, then open **Profile storage → Details → Copy regular Codex history**. Multi Codex copies local active and archived chats and local project information into that profile. Existing profile chats and credentials stay in place. Future chats remain separate, and the copy does not include cloud history or external project files.

## Install and update

Use the [install page](/install) or the Update button in Multi Codex. The Mac app requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later. The Mac app is ad-hoc signed without Apple notarization.
