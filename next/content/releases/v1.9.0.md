# Multi Codex v1.9.0

## Orbit in the Mac menu bar

One Orbit mascot opens a dropdown with your saved accounts and their avatars. Click an account to open it in Codex desktop. The account’s options button lists other installed launch targets.

The dropdown also has shortcuts to open Multi Codex, add an account, show or hide Orbit, open Orbit settings, and refresh usage.

Command-Q quits the launcher and leaves the mascot available for the rest of the login session. Choose **Quit Multi Codex** from the dropdown to close both. Open Multi Codex to start the mascot again.

## Platform requirements

The menu bar feature is available on Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later. Linux and Windows keep their existing launch and tray behavior.

## Updating

Use the Update button in Multi Codex or visit the [install page](/install). Existing profiles are available after the update.
